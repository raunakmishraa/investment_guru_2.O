Who Am I?

On A Mission To Transform
Retail Traders Into Market Masters

Hi, I'm Amit Jha, Founder & Director of Investment Guru, on a big mission—to help Nepal's retail investors and traders grow from uncertain market participants into disciplined, successful market leaders.

I started my journey exploring the financial markets and technical analysis in Nepal from the grassroots. Over the years, I noticed something vital—while large institutions had dedicated research desks, structured strategies, and risk controls, everyday retail investors in NEPSE struggled without authentic guidance, falling into traps of speculation and preventable losses. It didn't feel fair. So I set out to democratize practical, high-impact market education.

From our very first batch of learners to now mentoring thousands of active investors across Nepal, hosting The Amit Show, and conducting in-depth webinars and workshops, our focus has always remained straightforward: real market logic over guesswork.

Through our comprehensive trainings, like Technical Analysis Mastery and Complete Transformation, I want to make every Nepali investor financially confident, self-reliant, and consistently profitable.
